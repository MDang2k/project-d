## 2. The Scientist's arc

- **True identity:** Dr. Konrad Weiss, Project Ewigstein's deputy physicist on the Resonanz experiment. He fled in the 1965 evacuation chaos and has lived as "Kranz" since.
- **Act 1:** buys documents and translates them slowly; what he says is true but never the whole truth.
- **Act 2, mission 4 (Resonanz):** the logs carry his real name; he confesses that he helped draw the lock and believes the Cascade was his fault. From then on he pushes the player toward resealing it.
- **Act 3:** the only person able to read the Wardens' script (mission 8). His fate depends on the ending (part 4).

## 3. Project Ewigstein, 1965 (antagonists)

- **Professor Dr. Heinrich Albrecht:** project director who led the Resonanz; officially dead. He survived in the deep bunkers and is the mind of Kommando Resonanz, convinced he can bargain with the Prisoner. Weiss's former teacher.
- **Oberst Viktor Rauch:** commander of the project's security detachment, now Kommando Resonanz's military leader. Act 3 antagonist; boss candidate for the Cascade Site.
- **Other personnel:** names on the Project Ewigstein personnel roster (8d) are filled in by the writing pass.
- Depiction: villains with a fictional unit and insignia; no real Nazi symbols (item catalog and asset pipeline rule).

## 4. The player

A silent contractor. The chosen side sets callsign and briefings; no player voice or dialogue. Information is earned (GDD pillar 2).

## 5. Character presence in game

- Traders appear in the hub trader screens (portrait, name, short greeting lines); no 3D hub in the slice (hub is menu-based, GDD §4).
- Albrecht and Rauch appear in story missions and documents; Rauch can be a boss (8f data).
- The hollowed survivor in mission 3 and the survey team in mission 2 are scripted mission characters.

## Decisions

1. Six named traders with personalities and arc hooks.
2. The Scientist is Dr. Konrad Weiss under the alias Kranz; confesses at mission 4.
3. Albrecht leads Kommando Resonanz's thinking; Rauch leads its soldiers.
4. The player is a silent contractor.
