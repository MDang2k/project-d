# Story part 1: the core truth

Status: approved design (story bible, part 1 of 5). The hidden truth of Project D's setting. Players learn it only through play (knowledge items, story missions, late events). Later parts: 2 arc and story missions, 3 characters, 4 endings, 5 names and background. Extends GDD §3 and §14; the GDD timeline is updated in the same commit.

**Spoiler document.** Nothing here appears in player-facing text except through the reveals listed in part 2.

## The truth

- **The ruins are a prison.** Built in deep prehistory by an alien people, the **Wardens**, beneath what is now central Germany.
- **Ewigstein is the lock.** The crystals are not a power source by nature: they are the lattice of energy that holds the prison shut. Their "unlimited power" is the lock's strength; drawing on it weakens the lock. The frost around Ewigstein is the lock's cold, resisting.
- **The Prisoner** (working name) is a single vast intelligence sealed inside. It is never shown. Sealed, it can only reach out:
  - people exposed to its leaking influence are hollowed into the zombies (working name, post-slice);
  - the voices in the numbers-station broadcasts are its doing;
  - Project Ewigstein researchers began to hear it.
- **The Wardens** return late in the story to reseal the prison. They are hostile to every human, because humans broke the lock. They are the late-story aliens (GDD §3: not seen early).

## Timeline (true version)

| Year | Public history | Truth |
|---|---|---|
| 1939-1943 | the war proceeds | |
| 1943 | German engineers find "something" under central Germany | the outer ruins; crude salvage (prototype armor, power cells, relic weapons such as the StG 45) keeps the Reich fighting |
| 1943-1965 | twenty more years of stalemate | ruin salvage prolongs the war |
| 1950s | a secret weapons programme | the dig reaches the inner ruins and the Ewigstein lattice; Project Ewigstein begins |
| 1965 | the Cascade: a disaster of unknown cause destroys central Europe; the war ends | the "Resonanz" experiment: facing defeat, the Reich tried to draw the main lattice's full power at once for a war-winning weapon; the lock nearly failed and discharged as the Cascade; the Prisoner almost broke free; the lock held, weakened |
| 1965-1970 | Allies and Soviets jointly "contain" the zone with PMCs | both sides want Ewigstein for power, not knowing every shard taken weakens the lock |
| 1970 | the game begins | the lock is failing slowly; the Prisoner's reach is growing |

## What this means for gameplay and other specs

- Every Ewigstein delivery (8e) is, unknowingly, a step toward breaking the lock. The story should make the player feel this as they learn the truth (part 2 decides how and when).
- The three GDD endings gain moral weight: delivering the decisive Ewigstein to either government weakens the lock further; "destroy and seal" is the warden-aligned choice; the hidden fourth ending relates to the Wardens or the Prisoner (part 4).
- Existing content that already fits: the "Resonanz" lab note (8d), the numbers-station transcript (Hochwald), the frost-touched grove and frosted vehicles (outer foreshadowing), the containment breach in The Labs, the unknown script fragment (the Wardens' writing), the Cascade Site's deep vault.

## Decisions

1. The ruins are a Warden-built prison; Ewigstein is the lock.
2. One imprisoned intelligence, never shown, reaching out through leaks.
3. The war lasted longer because of early (1943) ruin salvage.
4. The Cascade was the Resonanz experiment drawing the whole lock.
5. The Wardens are the late aliens, hostile to all humans.
